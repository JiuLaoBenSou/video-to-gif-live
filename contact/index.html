Contact

Questions, bugs and ideas are welcome.

Email Foster Fei

The primary contact for this website is fanghong1994@gmail.com. Email is the best route for private support, privacy, content-removal and payment questions. Include enough information to identify the issue, but never send passwords or payment credentials. There is no fee to contact me and no fixed response-time guarantee.

Free feedback about the tool

Use the Requests & feedback form on the converter. You do not need an account or a payment. Include the browser and device you used, the step that failed, and any error message. Send text only; do not include a video, password, payment details or other private information.

Messages go to Foster Fei’s moderation queue. If approved, the nickname, category, message and date become public. A reply or publication is not guaranteed. For a comment removal or privacy request, start with “Please do not publish this request” and identify the public comment by its date, nickname and a short excerpt. Leave out sensitive information.

Optional support

Support on Ko-fi ↗

Ko-fi is an external creator profile and optional support service. Supporting the project is entirely optional. Use the email address above for contact; a donation is not required to ask a question.

Optional support and payment questions

Support is voluntary and does not purchase a conversion service. Ko-fi, Alipay and WeChat Pay handle their own payment flows; this website has no card checkout. For a payment or refund question, email the address above with the minimum transaction reference needed to locate it, and consult the relevant platform’s process. Refunds are reviewed individually; this page does not promise automatic refunds or a response deadline.

For copyright or content ownership concerns, email the public content’s URL, the material you own, the requested action and a way to reply. Do not post identity documents in public feedback. See the Privacy Policy for how feedback is handled.
