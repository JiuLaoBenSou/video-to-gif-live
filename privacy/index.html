WEBSITE INFORMATION · OCT 29, 2026

Privacy Policy

Your media stays on your device. Website visits and submitted feedback are handled separately.

Effective date: October 29, 2026. This policy applies to video2gifnlivephoto.com, operated by independent developer Foster Fei. The primary contact for privacy questions and removal requests is fanghong1994@gmail.com. Other options are described on the contact page.

1. Video and image processing

Files are not uploaded to our servers for conversion. Video decoding, frame extraction, GIF encoding and desktop Live Photo packaging take place locally in your browser. Media is held in browser memory and temporary local object URLs while you edit. Downloads are saved by your browser. Closing the page ends the editing session; copies you downloaded remain on your device until you delete them. We do not receive your video, its frames or your exported media through the conversion flow.

2. Cookies and Google advertising

This site uses Google AdSense advertising code. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites on the Internet. Advertising partners may also process device, browser, IP-address and ad-interaction information under their own policies. Local video processing does not mean that the whole website is cookie-free.

You can turn off personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. Read Google’s advertising technology policy, Google’s Privacy Policy, and how Google uses information from partner sites. Browser cookie controls and industry opt-out tools provide additional choices. Ad availability and personalization depend on Google’s settings, consent requirements and browser controls; the presence of code does not mean that Google has approved the site or delivered an ad.

3. Hosting, connection data and analytics

GitHub Pages hosts the static website and Cloudflare provides edge delivery and security. Requests therefore pass through infrastructure providers even when media conversion stays local. Connection information can include your IP address, requested URL, browser or user-agent information, timestamps, response status and approximate location derived from the connection. Cloudflare’s edge analytics summarize requests, traffic, errors and security events. These are server-side measurements, not a record of the video you selected.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is a separate product that uses a browser JavaScript beacon for page-view and performance measurements. This site’s application does not install that beacon or Google Analytics. Cloudflare security scripts may still run; those are distinct from the Web Analytics beacon. See Cloudflare Web Analytics and account and zone analytics.

Analytics retention: Cloudflare’s October 2, 2026 policy provides at least 30 days of analytics on every plan. Its adaptive HTTP-request, security-event and DNS datasets retain at least 31 days on Free and Pro domains, with up to 30 days queried in one request. Aggregated datasets keep their own per-plan limits. The exact dataset settings for this site have not been confirmed; a query window is not a deadline for deleting all underlying connection logs.

Other provider retention: Cloudflare security/connection logs outside those analytics datasets, GitHub hosting logs and Google advertising data are retained under the providers’ purposes, policies, account settings and applicable obligations. The operator has not confirmed one fixed period for these categories and does not promise a fixed deletion deadline. Refer to Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy and GitHub’s Privacy Statement, as well as Google’s policies above.

4. Anonymous text feedback and moderation

The optional feedback service runs on Cloudflare Workers and stores comments in Cloudflare D1. It receives the nickname you enter (or “Anonymous”), category, text, selected language and consent, and assigns a comment identifier, creation time and moderation status. Approved comments publish the nickname, category, text and date. Pending and rejected comments are not listed publicly. The endpoint accepts text JSON, not video uploads.

For rate limiting, the service uses your connection IP address to calculate a keyed, daily-changing hash. The comments table stores that hash rather than the raw IP address. Hashes attached to comments older than 24 hours are cleared when a later comment insertion runs; this is not a scheduled deletion job, so they can remain longer when there are no later submissions. The infrastructure provider can separately process connection metadata.

Feedback retention: there is no fixed automatic expiry for comment text, moderation records or their timestamps in the current service. Rejecting a comment hides it; it does not automatically delete the stored row. Ask about removal through the contact page, using only the public details needed to identify it. Requests require review; no automatic cleanup or deletion deadline is promised.

5. Optional support and your choices

If you email us, the sender address, message, attachments you choose to send and related correspondence are processed through the email service to handle your request. Email is separate from the converter: please do not email private media unless necessary for a support issue you choose to discuss. There is no fixed automatic expiry configured for support correspondence; provider retention and deletion policies also apply.

Opening Ko-fi, Alipay or WeChat Pay takes you to an external service or shows a payment QR code. We do not collect card details through the converter. Payment services may share supporter information with the creator under their own terms; read Ko-fi’s Privacy Policy and the relevant payment provider’s policy before paying. Support is optional, and private payment details should not be posted in public feedback.

You may use the conversion tool without submitting feedback or making a payment. You can clear browser data, control cookies, use the advertising choices above, and delete downloaded media locally. This policy may change as the service changes; the effective date will be updated. Provider-side retention settings remain unconfirmed as described above.
