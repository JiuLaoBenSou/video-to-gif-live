# Video → GIF / Live Photo

Website: https://video2gifnlivephoto.com/

Convert videos on your own device. Trim a clip and export a GIF or a silent Live Photo package. Videos are not uploaded.

- English by default. Chinese is available at /zh/. Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German are also available. The language selector keeps your edit in place; legacy ?lang=zh links are supported.
- GIF: original, 4K, 2K, 1080, 720, 480 and 360, preserving aspect ratio without upscaling; up to 15 seconds.
- GIF playback: 0.25x–4x, forward, reverse or ping-pong; a 1–30 FPS rotary dial. Up to 450 output frames, including slow motion and return frames.
- Target file size: 1 MB / 5 MB shortcuts or a custom 1 KB–64 MB limit (decimal units). Reduces colors and dimensions, keeps the selected frame rate and checks the final bytes. Reports failure if the target cannot be met. GIF exports are free, without added watermarks or sign-up.
- Live Photo: desktop Chrome or Edge, up to 3 seconds, without audio. Download the ZIP and follow its iPhone saving instructions. Mobile Live Photo saving is planned for a future app.
- Anonymous text requests are stored in Cloudflare D1 and published after moderation. The feedback service receives no video files.
- First-party task counts record fixed conversion outcomes and download triggers, never video content or filenames. Download triggers do not prove saving; see the Privacy Policy for retention and provider details.
- The illustrated How to use page includes original downloadable examples comparing a 1 MB target with a 5 MB target and their quality trade-offs.
- Optional support: Alipay / WeChat Pay in Chinese; https://ko-fi.com/fosterfei in English.
