GUIDE

How to convert a video to GIF

  1. Choose a local video

    Open an MP4, MOV or WebM file that your browser can play. The input limit is 200 MB. Your video stays on your device.

  2. Trim and adjust

    Drag the yellow handles to choose up to 15 seconds, or move the entire selection. Pick a size, turn the 1–30 FPS dial and choose forward, reverse or ping-pong playback. Speed ranges from 0.25× to 4×.

  3. Create, preview and download

    Create the GIF, preview the result and check its actual size before downloading. To fit an upload limit, enable Target file size; the tool reduces colors and dimensions while keeping your chosen FPS.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert MP4, MOV and WebM to GIF?

Yes, if your browser can decode the file. Codec support varies by browser and device; an H.264 MP4 is a useful alternative when a file cannot be opened.

How do I make a GIF smaller than 1 MB or 5 MB?

Enable Target file size and enter your limit in KB or MB. The supported range is 1 KB–64 MB, with 1 MB equal to 1,000,000 bytes. Compression can reduce image detail and resolution. If the limit cannot be met, increase it or shorten the clip.

Does it support portrait videos and 4K GIFs?

Portrait, landscape and square videos keep their aspect ratio. Choose Original, 4K, 2K, 1080, 720, 480 or 360; sizes above the source resolution are disabled. Large outputs may require a shorter clip or lower FPS to stay within processing limits.

Why is there no sound in my GIF?

GIF has no audio track. This tool exports silent GIFs and silent Live Photo packages. Slow motion and ping-pong add output frames; each GIF is limited to 450 frames and 64 MB.

How do I save a Live Photo to my iPhone?

Use desktop Chrome or Edge to create a silent clip up to 3 seconds. Download the ZIP containing LivePhoto.pvt, transfer it to your iPhone, extract it in Files and open the complete PVT package to save it to Photos. Browser support and the receiving device affect this workflow. Direct mobile Live Photo creation is planned for a future app.

Are videos uploaded to a server?

Conversion runs in your browser and video files are not uploaded. Feedback is sent only when you submit it. Advertising services may make separate network requests; see Privacy & data for details.
