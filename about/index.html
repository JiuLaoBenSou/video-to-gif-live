About

A small tool for the moments worth keeping.

I’m Foster Fei, an independent developer. I built Video → GIF / Live Photo to make a familiar task easier: choose a video, keep the part that matters, and turn it into a short looping image. The converter runs on your device. Your video does not need to be uploaded to a conversion service.

The GIF tool lets you trim a clip, keep its aspect ratio, choose a size and frame rate, change playback, and try a target file size. Desktop Chrome and Edge can also generate a short, silent Live Photo package. That is a separate export option, with its own limits and saving instructions. This is an independent project, with no affiliation to Apple or the browser makers.

The tool is free because I want it to be easy to try and useful for everyday work. Google advertising and optional support help with maintenance; neither is a promise of advertising approval or income. No payment unlocks conversion features. For questions, ideas or problems, use the contact options. You can also find my creator page at Ko-fi.
